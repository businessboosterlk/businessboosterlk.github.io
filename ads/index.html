Our view

Why tracking comes first.

An ad account without tracking spends money and reports activity: impressions, reach, clicks. None of it tells you if the phone rang.

We set up the pixel, the conversions and the lead form before the first campaign, so the very first report answers the only question that matters to an owner, which is what a customer cost.

What we will not do

We do not run ads on an account with no tracking. We fix that first, even if it delays the launch.

We do not add reach across platforms or across weeks. Reach counts people. The same person twice is still one person.

Questions we get

Paid ads questions we get on most calls.

What budget do I need?

Enough to buy a few dozen results of the kind you want in a month. We give you the number for your case on the call rather than print a figure here.

Meta or Google?

Google when people are already searching for what you sell. Meta and TikTok when they need to be shown it. Often both, in sequence.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your account as a partner and leave it intact if we ever part ways.

Can you just boost my posts?

We can. Usually we will talk you out of it. A proper ad set with one audience beats a boost nearly every time.
