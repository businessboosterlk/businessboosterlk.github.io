How it works

What the check-up asks and gives.

What does it ask?

Five questions: where your business stands online today, how enquiries arrive, what happens after an enquiry comes in, whether new customers can find you on Google and what matters most in the next six months.

What do I get?

A prescription: what to fix first, what can wait and why, with a link to the page that explains each fix.

How long does it take?

About a minute.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser. Nothing reaches us unless you press the button that sends your prescription on WhatsApp.

A prescription is only useful if someone fills it.

Send it over and a person talks it through with you. No obligation.
