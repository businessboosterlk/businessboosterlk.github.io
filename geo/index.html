What you get

Six things, each with a reason.

  • One clear identityThe same name, address, description and category everywhere the assistants read: your site, Google, directories and social profiles.
  • Citable pagesPages an assistant can quote in one sentence, with the fact and its source together.
  • Open doorsYour site tells the AI crawlers they are welcome and hands them a map.
  • Original materialAssistants prefer the source. Your own data, photographs and comparisons give them a reason to name you instead of the site that copied you.
  • Reputation signalsReviews and mentions on the sites the assistants trust.
  • A quarterly testWe ask the assistants the questions your customers ask and write down who they name.

Our view

What the assistants look for.

An assistant names a business when the same facts about it agree everywhere it looks: the website, the Google profile, the directories and the reviews.

One wrong phone number or two versions of the address is enough to make it hedge and name someone else. That is why GEO starts with housekeeping.

What we will not do

There is no paid placement inside ChatGPT or Gemini answers today. Anyone selling one is selling air.

We will not invent reviews or mentions. The assistants are better at spotting them than people are.

Questions we get

Asked on most calls.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT names us?

No. We can make you the clearest, most consistent, best supported answer in your category and test it every quarter.

Do I need SEO first?

Yes. The assistants lean on the same signals Google does. GEO without SEO is a roof without walls.

How do you measure it?

We ask the assistants your customers' questions, record the answers and compare quarter to quarter. It is a test, not a dashboard. We say so.
