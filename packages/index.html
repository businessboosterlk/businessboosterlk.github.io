Questions people ask before they say yes

Which package is likely to fit me?

Answer the three questions at the top of this page. If you are still unsure, tell us who handles your pages today and we will recommend one package. If none fits, we will say so.

What is the separate ad budget?

It is paid by you to Meta and Google to show your content to more people. It never passes through us. We recommend the minimum shown on each package.

How soon do we start?

Your first shoot is filmed within two weeks of the kickoff. Your first posts reach you for approval in the same fortnight.

What do you need from me?

One kickoff hour, access to your pages, a yes on each month's plan within three working days and someone we can reach on WhatsApp.

What happens if I dislike something?

It never goes out. Every post and video reaches you for approval first. We change it until you say yes.

Who owns the work?

You do. Everything made for you belongs to you. It stays in your Client Library.

Can I change package later?

Yes. Studio and Managed can move up or down at any month end. Apex is a six month partnership because the strategy needs time to compound.

How is performance measured?

Managed and Apex send a monthly report on what ran and how it performed on each platform. Apex also tracks enquiries in your Hub and agrees one business number each quarter. Revenue can only be reported when your team records which enquiries became sales.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Our crew films your team, your product and your place. If you do want to speak, we write the script to be spoken out loud and coach you on the day.
