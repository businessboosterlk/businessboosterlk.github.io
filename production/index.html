What you get

Six things in every month of social media.

  • FilmsReels, product films, founder pieces and the testimonial people actually watch. Scripted first, then shot, for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube.
  • PostsCarousels, single posts and stories built on a formula with a reason to exist: a product, a proof, a question your customers keep asking.
  • PhotographyProduct, people and place, shot on a planned day so one visit feeds a month of posts.
  • The monthly planYou see next month's plan on the twentieth. Every film and post on it has a hook, a formula and a job.
  • Shoot daysA checklist per shoot and a shot list per post. The shoot moves only with your yes.
  • CommunityWe watch the comments and messages and flag what needs an answer. You reply, in your own voice.

Community

We watch. You reply.

Every comment and message on your pages is read by a person on our team. Anything that needs an answer is flagged to you the same day with the context it came in. The reply comes from you, in your voice, because your customers can tell the difference and so can the platforms.

That is the whole of community management here. We do not answer for you and we do not run a chatbot in your name.

What we will not do

We do not reply to your customers for you. Community management here means we monitor and we flag. The answer comes from you, because a customer can tell.

We do not publish a result we have not measured. If a film did well you will see the number from the platform next to last month's, not a screenshot picked for the deck.

Questions we get

Social media questions we get on most calls.

Which platforms do you post on?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn, chosen by where your buyers spend their time. The plan says which platform each piece is for.

How many films and posts a month?

It depends on the package and the business. We agree the count before we start and you see it on the plan every month. Fewer pieces with a job beat a daily post nobody asked for.

Do you work in Sinhala and Tamil?

Yes. Several of our clients post in Sinhala or Tamil first and English second. The plan says which language each piece is in.

Who owns the footage?

You do. Raw footage and finished files live in your Hub library and you keep them if we ever part ways.

Can you use my existing photos?

If they are good enough to sell with, yes. Usually the first shoot day pays for itself in the posts it feeds.

What decides the price?

How many films and posts a month, how many shoot days and whether we also run your ads. We agree a monthly scope in writing before we start; the price comes on WhatsApp, not on this page.
