What you get

Six things in every month of SEO.

  • The technical foundationFast pages, clean structure, a title and a description on every page, the data search engines read. Checked with Google's own tools, not a plugin.
  • The words people typeThe questions your customers actually ask, in English, Sinhala and Tamil where it matters, mapped to pages.
  • Content that earns its placeBlog posts and service pages written to answer a question properly. Four a month on a web retainer, each with a job.
  • Local searchYour Google Business Profile set up and kept alive, because for a Colombo business that is often the first result.
  • Mentions earnedLinks from real sites with a genuine reason to mention you, such as a supplier, a trade body or the local paper that covered your opening. Never a bought list.
  • The monthly reportImpressions, clicks and positions from Search Console, compared with last month.

Our view

SEO problems are rarely a missing trick.

They are about the foundation: a page that is slow on a phone, titles copied from page to page and the questions customers actually type answered nowhere on the site.

Fix that first. Rankings follow the foundation, never the other way round. A site that answers its customers properly tends to keep its place long after a trick has stopped working for everyone who bought it.

Questions we get

SEO questions we get on most calls.

How long until SEO works?

Months, not weeks. We say so up front. Technical fixes show within weeks and new pages take longer to earn their place.

Do I need a blog?

If you want to rank for questions, yes. A service page answers one question. A blog answers the fifty around it.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets you found on Google. AEO gets your answer quoted in the answer box and by voice assistants. GEO gets you named when someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini. We do all three from the same content.

Can you do SEO on my existing site?

Usually, yes. The scan tells us how much of the foundation is there before anyone commits.
