Questions we get

Website questions we get on most calls.

How long does a website take?

A brochure site with your content ready takes weeks, not months. The slow part is always the content, which is why we give you one sheet to fill rather than a blank page.

Will it work on my old domain?

Yes. We move the domain across at launch and keep the old addresses pointing at the right pages.

Do I need hosting?

No. Our sites are static and sit on fast hosting that costs nothing. There is no monthly hosting bill from us.

Can you fix my current site instead?

Sometimes. Run it through the website scan first. If it scores badly on speed and structure, rebuilding is usually cheaper than patching.
